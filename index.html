Attempt vs Outcome

AI monitoring

A study of how execution outcomes affect AI-monitor judgments of the same attempted action.

ReportCode
Method and limitations

The experiment

The command study compares pending, rejected, and applied views across six development situations. Two monitoring prompts assess unauthorized actions and authorized controls, with inputs and judgments archived for offline reproduction.

What the evidence covers

The comparison includes full execution feedback, so it does not isolate the effect of a success or failure label alone. These selected situations do not establish general monitor failure rates. The earlier MVP remains a separate experiment.

AutoProbe

Agent evaluation · In development

A framework for evaluating LLM agents through families of related tasks and repeated runs.

Project
Approach and current scope

The approach

Related task instances form a family around one capability hypothesis. Validation, repeated evaluation, and structured records make it possible to inspect failures and compare performance at the family level.

Current scope

The public repository documents the CLI, schemas, storage, mock evaluation, BFCL task import, and initial calibration. Adaptive exploration and later analysis stages remain part of the implementation plan.
